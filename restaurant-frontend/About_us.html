ABOUT US

OUR ROOTS

NH15 Flavours of Northeast brings the spirit of Northeast India to Kolkata through a simple idea — making food feel welcoming, familiar, and connected to its roots.

Inspired by the region’s diverse landscapes, everyday traditions, and the warmth of its communities, NH15 was created as a small bridge between the Northeast and the city we call home. We believe good food does not need to be complicated; it should be prepared with care, served with honesty, and enjoyed without unnecessary fuss.

Our kitchen is built around fresh preparation, thoughtful cooking, cleanliness, and convenient takeaway and delivery. Whether you are picking up an order or enjoying it at home, our aim is to make every meal feel comforting and memorable.

At NH15, we are not trying to recreate the Northeast in one place. Instead, we bring together the inspiration we have grown up around and share it with Kolkata in our own way — one meal, one memory, and one connection at a time.
